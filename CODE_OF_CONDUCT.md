# Code of conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Show empathy towards other community members.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks,
publishing others' private information without permission, and other conduct
which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report incidents to the repository owner through GitHub. Maintainers will
review reports promptly, respect the reporter's privacy, and apply
consequences proportionate to the behaviour, up to a permanent ban from the
project spaces.
